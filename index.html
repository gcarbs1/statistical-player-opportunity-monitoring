Who gets a chance before 24?

Round by round, this tracks the playing time of 18,681 players aged 16 to 23 across 16 top-flight leagues, using Transfermarkt data from 2019 to 2026. Each selection decision is compared with players of the same league, season and age, and the result becomes a continuous opportunity signal that shows when a young player gains, holds or loses a place. The signal describes coaches’ choices, not talent or performance.

04

Half of all early watches come within 180 minutes

An early watch flags the first unusually fast rise in opportunity: two of up to three consecutive decisions with a residual of at least +0.75. 8,617 players had one; the median comes at 20.9 years, after 10 decisions.
